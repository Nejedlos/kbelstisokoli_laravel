# First steps in the system

Welcome to the new information system of the Kbelští sokoli club. This system will help you with everything you need for your sports life in the club.

## Login
For your first login, use the e-mail address you provided in your application.

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter your details.
3. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link.

## Profile settings
We recommend checking your contact details in the My Profile section.
- Telephone
- Address
- Health insurance company
