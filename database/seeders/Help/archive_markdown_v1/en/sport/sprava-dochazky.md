## Screen Overview

The **Attendance Management** section is a key tool for every coach. After entering this section, you will see a clear table of your team's training sessions and matches for the current week.

At the top of the screen is the **Filter Panel**, which allows you to switch between teams (if you manage more than one) and change the time period. In the main table, you see the date, time, event name, and current attendance status for each event.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Attendance recording should ideally take place immediately after the event. Follow these steps:

1.  In the event list, click the **Record Attendance** button for a specific training session.
2.  A list of all players nominated for the event will open.
3.  For each player, select one of the options:
    -   **Present**: The player actively participated in the event.
    -   **Excused**: The player properly apologized in advance.
    -   **Unexcused**: The player did not arrive and did not let you know.
    -   **Late**: The player arrived, but with a significant delay.
4.  After marking all players, click the **Save Attendance** button at the bottom of the page.

## Actions and Buttons

In addition to the recording itself, you can use these advanced features:

-   **Bulk Excuse**: If a practice is cancelled, you can excuse all players at once with one click.
-   **Export to PDF**: Generates a printed report of attendance for the selected month (suitable for subsidy purposes).
-   **Calendar Sync**: A link to subscribe to attendance data on your mobile phone.

## Common Mistakes

> **WARNING:** If you forget to save the attendance, the system will mark it as "incomplete" after 24 hours and send you an automatic reminder.

## Tips and Best Practices

> **TIP:** To speed up work in the field, we recommend using the mobile version of the website. The table is optimized for touch control and allows for quick "checking off" of participants right on the court.
