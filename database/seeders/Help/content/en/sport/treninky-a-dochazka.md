Maintaining precise attendance in the **Trainings** section is one of the main pillars of coaching work in the club. Attendance data affects player evaluations, coach rewards, and child motivation.

### Planning the Training Plan
In the Kbelští sokoli system, you plan trainings as individual events:
1. **Team Assignment**: A training can be shared by multiple teams (e.g., Men A and Men B). The system automatically merges the rosters of these teams for attendance purposes.
2. **Location and Time**: Selection of the hall (RumcajsArena Kbely, Třinecká, etc.) and the exact time.
3. **Regularity**: The system allows for quick copying of already created trainings, which speeds up the creation of the plan for the whole month ahead.
4. **Color Coding**: In the training table, future events are highlighted with a green background color so you can easily distinguish them from history.

### Excusal and Confirmation Process
Players and parents use the mobile app to interact with trainings:
- **Planned**: The player sees the training in the calendar and can pre-confirm participation or excuse themselves.
- **Types of Excuses**: Players can add a reason for the excuse (e.g., Illness, School, Work). The coach sees these reasons in the attendance detail under the player's name.
- **Notifications**: If a player excuses themselves less than 2 hours before the start, the system may mark it as a "late excuse".

### Taking Attendance and the Mismatch System
The coach records the actual status after (or during) the training:
- **Present** (Attended) / **Absent** (Absent) / **Excused** (Excused).
- **Mismatch (Discrepancy)**: If a player confirmed participation but did not show up, a **red badge with the count of discrepancies** appears for the record in the training table. This is a key tool for checking discipline.
- **Quick Action**: In the training detail, you can use the bulk action "All Present" and then manually excuse only those who were missing.

### Attendance Reports and Statistics
From the collected data, the system automatically generates reports:
- **Team Attendance**: Percentage expression of attendance for the entire period (month/season), which coaches see in the Teams section.
- **Individual Attendance**: Each player (or parent) sees their current balance in their profile compared to the team average.
- **Hours Worked**: For coaches, this data serves as a basis for calculating rewards for trained hours.

### Tips for Coaches
- **Training Notes**: In the "Note" field in the training detail, record the focus (e.g., zone defense) so that players in the mobile app know what to prepare for.
- **Bulk Excusal**: If a training is cancelled due to a hall failure, use the bulk action "Excuse All", which sends a notification of the cancellation to all players.
