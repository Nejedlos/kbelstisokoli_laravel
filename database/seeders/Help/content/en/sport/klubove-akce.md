The **Club Events** section (Sports Agenda > Club Events) is used to organize events that do not fall under the standard training or match process.

### Types of Club Events
The system distinguishes several categories of events, which differ in color and purpose:
- **<i class="fa-light fa-campground text-warning"></i> Camp / Retreat**: Major off-season sporting events. They have their own record of finance and accommodation.
- **<i class="fa-light fa-users-rectangle text-info"></i> Meeting / Session**: Intended for organizational committees, coaching boards, or general meetings.
- **<i class="fa-light fa-hand-holding-heart text-primary"></i> Volunteer / Community Event**: Hall maintenance, help with organizing tournaments, etc.
- **<i class="fa-light fa-glass-cheers text-success"></i> Social Event**: Club parties, anniversary celebrations, joint visits to A-team matches.

### Creating and Configuring an Event
When creating a new event, you define its scope and interactivity:
1. **Team Assignment**: The event can concern specific teams or "the entire club" (if you do not select any team).
2. **Visibility (is_public)**: Public events are automatically displayed on the club's website in the calendar for the general public. **New events are private by default (off).**
3. **RSVP / Attendance**: If you turn on the "Enable Attendance" option, players in the mobile app will see a button to sign up, and coaches can keep track of those present.

### Working with Participants (RSVP)
For each event, you will find a **Participants** tab in the detail.
- **Registration**: Players can confirm participation (Yes / No / Maybe).
- **Capacity**: A maximum number of participants can be set (if the system supports limits).
- **Export**: The list of registered participants can be exported for attendance on paper (e.g., for camps).

### Finance and Payments for Events
For events of the "Camp" or "Retreat" type, a participation fee is often required.
- **Link to Finance**: The system allows for bulk generation of payment prescriptions to all confirmed event participants.
- **Variable Symbol**: Each event can have its specific prefix for payments, which facilitates pairing.

### Tips for Organizers
- **Location with Map**: For events outside the Kbely hall (e.g., a retreat in the mountains), insert precise GPS coordinates so that participants can open navigation in their mobile app.
- **Notifications (Push)**: After creating an important event (e.g., a general meeting), we recommend using the "Announcements" module for bulk notification with a link to the event detail.
