# Planning and Starting a New Season

Seasons in the Kbelští sokoli system serve to separate data across years, especially for financial and sports evidence.

## Creating a New Season
1. Go to the **Seasons** section.
2. Click on **New Season**.
3. Enter the name (e.g., 2025/2026) and the start and end dates.
4. Set the season as **Active** to make it the default for all new records.

## Initializing Configurations
This step is the most important when transitioning to a new season:
1. On the list of seasons in the table header, click the **Initialize Configurations** action.
2. This action goes through all active members from the previous season and creates a record for them in the new season.
3. This carries over their set tariffs and payment rules, enabling bulk generation of prescriptions for the new year.

## Tips
- **Archiving**: After the end of a season, deactivate it so it doesn't get in the way of daily work, but the data will remain in the system for historical overviews.
- **Rosters**: After season initialization, it's necessary in the Teams section to assign teams to the new season and update their rosters.
