# Club Competitions

This section is used to organize and manage all competitions in which the club participates or hosts. Proper configuration of a competition is essential for displaying results, standings, and schedules on the website.

### Competition Types
When creating a competition, you must select its type:
1. **Official ČBF:** Competitions under the Czech Basketball Federation. These are synchronized with external data (e.g., results, rosters).
2. **Own Tournaments:** Events hosted directly by our club (e.g., Christmas tournament, summer camp). Results and matches are entered manually here.
3. **Other:** Friendly matches or unofficial tournaments hosted by other clubs.

### Website Settings
In the competition details, you can control how it's presented to the public:
- **Show on Homepage:** Adds information about the competition to the news section or the competition overview on the main page.
- **Recruitment Page:** If the competition is intended for beginners, mark it for display in the recruitment section.
- **Competition Logo:** Uploading a logo (e.g., the ČBF logo or tournament logo) significantly enhances the visual presentation of match schedules on the website.

### Seasonal Link
Each competition is strictly tied to a specific **season** (e.g., 2025/2026).
- After the season ends, the competition automatically moves to the archives.
- If a competition continues in the next season, a new record must be created for it under the new season (or use the season renewal tool).

### Frequently Asked Questions
- **Why can't I see ČBF results?** Check if the competition is set to type `cbf` and has a valid `external_id`.
- **How do I add a team to a competition?** Teams are assigned to competitions in the Team section through the roster settings for that specific competition.
