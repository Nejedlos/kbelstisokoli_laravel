# Opponent Directory

The **Opponents** section serves as a central database of clubs against which Kbelští sokoli play matches.

## Managing Opponents
1. Open the **Opponents** section.
2. Click on **New Opponent**.
3. Fill in the club **Name** and the **City** where they are based.
4. You can add a link to the **Logo** (to be displayed on the web).

## Resolving Duplicates
Automatic imports from cz.basketball may result in duplicate records (e.g., "BC Slaný" and "ŠBK Slaný").

To maintain database integrity:
1. In the opponent list, a **Merge Suggestions** widget is displayed in the top right.
2. The widget lists pairs of opponents with similar names.
3. Use the **Merge** action to select which record is correct, and all matches will be transferred to it.

## Tips
- **Logos**: For the best appearance on the web, use opponent logos with a transparent background (PNG format).
- **Search**: The opponent table allows quick searching by name and city.
