# Team and Roster Management

Team management is the cornerstone of our sports agenda. In this section, you define the team's composition, its visual identity, and technical parameters for competitions.

### Visual Identity (Team Branding)
Each team can have its own "face" within the KS system:
- **Team Logo:** If a team has its own logo (e.g., in combined teams with another club), upload it in the team details.
- **Team Colors:** You can set primary and secondary colors, which will be reflected in the match graphics on the website.
- **Team Photo:** A current photo of the entire team is displayed on the team profile on the public website.

### Rosters
A roster determines which players and coaches belong to a team in a given season.
- **Adding a Member:** In the "Team Members" tab, use the **Attach Member** action. The player must already be in the user database.
- **Team Roles:** You can assign a role to each roster member (Player, Captain, Head Coach, Assistant).
- **Jersey Numbers:** You can track the jersey number for players for the specific season.

### Synchronization and Competitions
A team is typically part of one or more competitions.
- **External ID:** If a team plays in a ČBF competition, its `external_id` must be filled out. Without it, automatic downloading of results and standings will not function.
- **Competitions:** In the team details, you can see which competitions the team is registered for in the current season.

### Training and Attendance Management
The team serves as a filter for the training process.
- When creating training, you select one or more teams.
- Roster members of these teams will automatically appear in the attendance overview for that training.

### Coach Tips
- **Regular Maintenance:** Before each season starts, check the roster and remove players who are leaving the team.
- **Update Photos:** Update the team photo at least once a year, ideally after receiving new jerseys.
- **VS Verification:** Financial charges are often generated based on team affiliation, so always keep your roster up-to-date.
