# Rosters and Team Membership

This article describes how to manage the player list in individual teams and how to set their playing status.

## Adding a Player to a Team
Assigning players to a team takes place in the team details on the **Players** card.

1. Open the details of the selected team.
2. Go to the **Players** tab.
3. Click the **Add Player** (Attach) button.
4. Search for the player by name.
5. Set the membership parameters:
    - **Role in Team**: Player, Captain, or Assistant.
    - **Primary Team**: Check if this is the player's main team (affects jersey color in some overviews).
    - **Player is on Roster**: A key flag for displaying the player in match nominations.

## Roster Management
If a player trains with the team but does not play in matches, leave the **Player is on Roster** box unchecked. Such a player will appear in training attendance but will not be offered for game nominations.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can a player be in multiple teams?** Yes, a player can be a member of any number of teams.
- **How do I remove a player from a team?** In the players table next to a specific name, click the **Remove** (Detach) action. This will only cancel the link to the team; the player profile will remain preserved.
