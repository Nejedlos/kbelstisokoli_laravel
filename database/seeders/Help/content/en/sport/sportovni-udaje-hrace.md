# Player Sports Data

In the Kbelští sokoli system, every user with a sports profile has a **Player** tab in their details. This tab serves to record important data for coaches and administration.

## Sports Card Content

### Basketball Data
- **Jersey Number**: Currently assigned number that appears on rosters.
- **Position**: Selection of playing position (e.g., PG, C).
- **Dominant Hand**: Information for coaches regarding athletic development.
- **ČBF License**: Official basketball federation license number.

### Physical Parameters and Equipment
- **Height and Weight**: Important for tracking growth and for nominations.
- **Jersey and Shorts Size**: Used for ordering and managing club apparel.

### Internal Information (for Coaches only)
- **Medical Note**: Record of health restrictions, allergies, or validity of medical check-ups.
- **Coach Note**: Internal evaluation or insights regarding the player.

## Player Photos
At the bottom of the tab is a gallery for uploading photos:
- Photos uploaded here are displayed on the player's profile on the web.
- **Important**: The first photo in the sequence is considered primary and will be used on the team roster.

## Tips
- **Updates**: We recommend updating the player's height and weight at least once every six months.
- **Privacy**: Data in the "Internal Information" section is visible only to administrators and coaches; regular users do not have access to it.
