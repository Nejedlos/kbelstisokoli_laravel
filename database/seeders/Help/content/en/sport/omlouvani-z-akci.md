# Excusing from Events

This manual is primarily for players and parents who need to excuse themselves from a training or a match.

## How to Excuse Yourself
1. Log in to the **Member Section** on the web or via the mobile app.
2. On the dashboard or in the calendar, click on the specific event (Training / Match).
3. In the event details, you will see a button for **Confirm Participation** or **Excuse**.
4. If you are excusing yourself, select a **Reason for Excuse** (e.g., Illness, School, Family reasons) and optionally add a short comment.

## Excusal Rules
- **Deadline**: Each team may have a set time limit for when it is possible to excuse oneself (e.g., 24 hours before the event).
- **Late Excuse**: Once the deadline has passed, excusing yourself in the system is no longer possible, and you must contact the coach directly (by phone / SMS).
- **Recurring Events**: If you know of a long-term absence (e.g., spa), inform the coach so they can adjust your attendance in bulk.

## What do the Colors in the Calendar Mean?
- **Blue**: Event is waiting for your response.
- **Green**: Your participation is confirmed.
- **Gray**: You are excused.
- **Orange/Red**: Late excuse or unexcused absence.

## FAQ
- **Can a parent excuse me?** Yes, if the parent has a profile in the system linked with their child, they can perform excuses for them.
- **Where can I see my excuses?** In the My Profile section on the Attendance tab, you will find a complete overview of your confirmations and excuses.
