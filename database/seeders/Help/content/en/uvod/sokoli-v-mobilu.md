## Purpose
Our system is fully optimized for mobile phones and can be installed directly onto your home screen as an app (PWA) without having to download anything from the App Store or Google Play.

## Advantages of the Home Screen App
- **Quick Access:** You don't have to enter the address into the browser every time.
- **Larger Screen:** The app opens without the browser's address bar, which saves space.
- **Speed:** The system behaves more smoothly on a mobile phone.

## How to Add the System to Your Home Screen

### Android (Google Chrome)
1. Open the address `https://kbelstisokoli.cz/admin` in the Chrome browser.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Click on the three dots icon (menu) at the top right.
4. Select the **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App** option.
5. Confirm the name "Sokoli" and save.

### iOS / iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the address `https://kbelstisokoli.cz/admin` in the Safari browser.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Click on the share icon (square with an up arrow) in the bottom bar.
4. Scroll down and select the **Add to Home Screen** option.
5. Confirm the name and save.

## What if I don't see the option?
- Make sure you are using the default browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone).
- If you are on the page in private (incognito) mode, the installation option may not be available.
- Try refreshing the page (Reload).
