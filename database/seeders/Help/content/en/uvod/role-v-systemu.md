## Purpose
The "Sokoli" system uses various user roles to ensure that everyone sees exactly the information they need for their activities within the club.

## Overview of Roles

### 1. Player / Member
Basic role for every athlete. Allows:
- Managing your own profile.
- Following the team schedule (practices, matches).
- Excusing oneself from events.
- Overview of one's own payments.

### 2. Parent
Role for legal representatives of children. Allows:
- Everything the Player role does, but for their children's accounts.
- Switching between children's profiles.
- Payment of membership fees for children.

### 3. Coach
Role for managing the sporting agenda. Allows:
- Managing rosters for one's teams.
- Planning practices and matches.
- Managing attendance.
- Communication with players and parents.

### 4. Administrator / Treasurer
Role for club management. Allows:
- Managing all members and teams.
- Financial management of the club (fees, banking).
- System and website settings.

## Role FAQs
- **Can I have multiple roles?** Yes, the system allows a combination of roles (e.g., a Coach who is also a Parent).
- **Who assigns roles?** Roles are assigned by the club's lead administrator based on your function.
- **I don't see a section I should see?** Try logging out and logging back in. If the problem persists, contact the administrator.

## Security Notice
Roles with higher permissions (Coach, Admin) require active two-factor authentication (2FA) to access data.
