## Purpose
The security of your data is a priority for us. In addition to a strong password, our system offers the option of two-factor authentication (2FA), which ensures that no unauthorized person can access your account, even if they know your password.

## What is 2FA?
When logging in, in addition to your password, you will also have to enter a unique code that is generated for you in a mobile application (e.g., Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator).

## How to Set Up 2FA
1. Go to the **My Profile** -> **Security** section.
2. Click the **Activate two-factor authentication** button.
3. A QR code will appear on the screen. Scan this code using the app on your mobile phone.
4. Confirm activation by entering the code from the app into the system.
5. **IMPORTANT:** Download and securely store the backup codes (Recovery Codes) that appear after activation. These codes will allow you to log in if you lose access to your phone.

## Common Mistakes
- **Losing your phone:** If you don't have access to your phone or backup codes, you won't be able to log in. In that case, you must contact the system administrator to manually reset your 2FA.
- **Time difference:** If the app generates codes that the system doesn't accept, check if your phone has the correct and automatically synchronized time set.

## Obligation for Administrators
For security reasons, two-factor authentication is **mandatory** for all users with the role of coach and administrator. Without setting up 2FA, you will not be able to work with club data.
