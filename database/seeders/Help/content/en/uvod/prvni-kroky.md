Welcome to the new information system of the **Kbelští Sokoli** basketball club. This article will guide you through the initial steps from logging in to basic navigation in the administration.

## 1. Entering the Locker Room (Log In)
It all starts on the login page, which we call **"Vstup do kabiny"** (Entering the Locker Room).

*   **Email:** Use the address you provided when registering with the club.
*   **Password:** For your first login, use the password sent to you in the activation email.
*   **Forgot password:** If you don't remember your password, click the password recovery link. A reset link will be sent to you within a few minutes.

## 2. Dashboard (Nástěnka)
After a successful login, you will see the **Dashboard** (Nástěnka). It is your tactical board where you will find the most important information:

*   **Quick Actions:** Buttons at the top to instantly create a match, training session, or a new user (depending on your role).
*   **KPI Statistics:** An overview of upcoming events – matches and training sessions that await you in the near future.
*   **Financial Overview:** If you have the appropriate permissions, you will see the status of receivables and an overview of payments for the last month.

## 3. Main Navigation
In the left menu, you will find a crossroads to all parts of the system:
*   **Sports Agenda:** Management of teams, matches, and training sessions.
*   **People and Members:** Evidence of players and their profiles.
*   **Economy:** Everything related to membership fees and payments.
*   **Content and Media:** Editing web pages and photo galleries.

## 4. Member Section and Web
Directly from the administration, you can switch to the public website or the member section using the links in the top bar.

> [!TIP]
> **Always up-to-date data:** Please check your "My Profile" section to ensure your contact details are current. A correct email and phone number are crucial for club communication.
