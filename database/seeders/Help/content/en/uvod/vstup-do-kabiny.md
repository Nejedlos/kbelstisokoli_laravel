## Screen Overview
The entrance to the club's system is located at `https://kbelstisokoli.cz/admin`. The page is designed in club colors with a prominent logo and a basketball motif in the background.

### Main Login Elements
1.  **Field "Username / E-mail":** Enter the e-mail address you provided to the club during registration.
2.  **Field "Password":** Enter your access password.
3.  **Button "Vstup do kabiny" (Enter Locker Room):** Confirm and log in to the system.
4.  **Link "Zapomenutá nahrávka?" (Forgot Pass?):** Used for password recovery if you've forgotten it.

## Step-by-Step Guide
1. Go to `https://kbelstisokoli.cz/admin`.
2. Fill in your e-mail and password.
3. If you are logging in from your private device, you can leave the "Remember me" box checked.
4. Click on **Vstup do kabiny**.
5. If you have two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, you will be prompted in the next step to enter a code from your mobile app.

## Common Mistakes
- **Špatná nahrávka! (Bad Pass!):** This message appears if you enter an incorrect combination of e-mail or password. Check if *CapsLock* is on.
- **Přešlap (Overstepping):** If you try to access a section for which you do not have permission, the system will return you back.
- **Expired Session:** For security reasons, the system will log you out after a long period of inactivity. Just log in again.

## Efficiency Tip
We recommend saving the login page in your browser's bookmarks or adding it to your phone's home screen as an app (see the **Sokoli v mobilu** guide).
