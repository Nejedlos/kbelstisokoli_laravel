## Purpose
This article summarizes the most frequently asked questions and answers for new users of the "Sokoli" system. If you don't find an answer here, please contact your coach or the club secretary.

## General Questions

### How do I register?
Self-registration in the system is not possible. A new user (player or parent) is created by the club administrator based on a membership application. You will then receive an e-mail with an invitation to the system.

### The system tells me "Špatná nahrávka!" (Bad Pass!), what should I do?
This message means incorrect login details. Verify that you don't have a typo in your e-mail address and that you are entering the correct password (watch out for upper/lowercase letters).

### I didn't receive the password reset e-mail.
Check your "Spam" or "Promotions" folders. Some mail servers may filter out automated e-mails from the system. If the e-mail doesn't arrive within 15 minutes, try requesting a "pass" (reset link) again.

## Account and Profile Management

### Can I change my e-mail address?
Yes, you can edit your e-mail address in the **My Profile** section. The change will take effect immediately upon saving.

### Why can't I change my personal ID number or name?
Data linked to the Czech Basketball Federation (ČBF) register are locked for manual editing. If there is an error in them, please send us a message, and we will correct it in the register.

### How do I set up my child's account?
As a parent, you have access to your children's profiles directly from your account. At the top of the screen or in your profile, you will find a list of children you can switch between.

## Technical Questions

### Does the system work without internet?
No, an active internet connection is necessary to view current information and excuse oneself from events.

### Is the system secure?
Yes, all communication is encrypted. For higher security, we recommend that all users (and mandatory for administrators) activate two-factor authentication (2FA).
