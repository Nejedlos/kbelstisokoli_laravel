## Purpose
The "My Profile" section serves as your business card in the club. It is important that your data is up-to-date so that we can communicate with you in a timely manner.

## Screen Overview
The section is divided into several logical blocks:
1.  **Personal Information:** Name, surname, date of birth, and possibly address.
2.  **Contact Information:** E-mail address and phone number.
3.  **Club Data (ReadOnly):** Data from the register (e.g., ID number, ČBF registration number) that you cannot change yourself.
4.  **Change Password:** Option to set a new access password.
5.  **Photo (Avatar):** Upload your profile photo, which is displayed to coaches in the roster.

## How to Edit Information
1. In the side menu, click the **Profile** item.
2. Edit the required fields (e.g., new phone number).
3. Click the **Save Changes** button.

## Common Mistakes
- **Fields cannot be edited:** Some fields are locked for editing for security and legislative reasons (GDPR, ČBF register). If there is an error in them, please contact the club secretary.
- **No photo uploaded:** Coaches appreciate it if you have an up-to-date profile photo. It makes it easier for them to orient themselves in the roster (especially with new children).

## Tip for Parents
If you have multiple children in the club, you will see a list of all children's accounts you manage under your profile. You can switch between them by clicking on their name.
