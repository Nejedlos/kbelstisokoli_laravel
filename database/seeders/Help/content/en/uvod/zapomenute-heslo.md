## Purpose
If you don't remember your access password, you can reset it yourself using the e-mail address registered in the system.

## Step-by-Step Guide
1. On the login page, click the **Zapomenutá nahrávka?** (Forgot Pass?) link.
2. Enter your e-mail address.
3. Click the button to send the reset request.
4. Within a few minutes, you will receive an e-mail with a link to set a new password.
5. Click the link in the e-mail and enter your new password on the page that opens (twice for confirmation).
6. Confirm the change and then log in with your new password.

## Common Mistakes
- **Email not delivered:** If the e-mail doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your "Spam" or "Promotions" folder.
- **Invalid link:** The link in the e-mail has limited validity (usually 60 minutes). If you don't use it in time, you must request a new "pass" (reset link).
- **Entering the wrong e-mail:** If you enter an e-mail that does not exist in our system, for security reasons, nothing will be sent. Verify that you are using the same e-mail to which you receive club information.

## Security
Your new password should be unique and strong enough (combination of letters, numbers, and symbols). Never use the same passwords as for other services.
