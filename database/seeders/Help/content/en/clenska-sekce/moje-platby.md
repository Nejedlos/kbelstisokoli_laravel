# My Payments and Invoicing

In this section, you will find an overview of all your financial obligations to the club (Charges) and records of payments you have already made.

## Where to Find My Finance

You can find the finance overview after logging into the member section in the menu under the **Finance** item (or in your profile details).

## How to Pay

For each unpaid charge (status "To be paid"), you will see:
-   **Amount** to be paid.
-   **Bank account** of the club.
-   **Variable Symbol (VS):** The most important information for correct payment matching.
-   **Due Date:** By when the amount should be sent.

## Statuses of My Charges

-   **To be paid / Overdue:** The charge is waiting for your payment.
-   **Partially Paid:** You have already sent a part, but the rest remains to be paid.
-   **Paid:** Everything is fine, the payment has been received and matched.

## What to do when...

### ...I have paid, but the system still shows "To be paid"?
Processing a payment can take a few days. The administrator must record the payment from the bank and match it with your charge. If the status lasts longer than a week, contact the club management.

### ...I forgot to provide the Variable Symbol?
In this case, the system cannot automatically recognize the payment. Inform the club treasurer, providing the payment date and amount so they can find it manually.

### ...I want to pay in cash?
Contact the treasurer or your coach. Upon receipt of cash, they will issue a payment confirmation in the system for you.

> [!TIP]
> We recommend setting up a standing order for regular monthly fees. The VS usually remains the same for a given type of payment.
