# My Attendance and Excuses

In the member section, you have a complete overview of your sports program. This article will explain how to track upcoming events and how to properly excuse yourself.

## How do I see my program?
In the **[My Program](/member/attendance)** section, you will find an overview of all training sessions, matches, and club events that concern your teams.
- **Upcoming:** Events that are yet to happen.
- **Past:** History of your participation.

## How to excuse yourself from a training?
If you know you won't make it to a training, it's your duty to excuse yourself as soon as possible.
1. Go to the **My Program** section.
2. Find the relevant training session.
3. Click the **Excuse** button.
4. Enter the reason for absence (e.g., illness, school, family reasons).
5. Confirm the excuse.

The coach will see your excuse immediately and can better plan the training session.

## Excuses from matches
Excusing yourself from a match is even more critical than from a training. For matches, coaches work with a **nomination**.
- If you are nominated, confirm your participation as soon as possible.
- If you cannot participate for serious reasons, use the same procedure as for training.

## Attendance Statistics
You can track your historical attendance and "percentage morale" in the **[My Statistics](/member/statistics/me)** section. Good attendance is often a key factor for nomination for important matches!
