# Club Finance System

Welcome to the overview of the Kbelští sokoli club finance system. This module is used for transparent management of membership fees, camp fees, tournaments, and other sports activities.

## Basic Concepts

To make the system work correctly, we distinguish three key elements:

1.  **Finance Charge (Debt):** This is information about **how much and for what** a member should pay. A charge has its title (e.g., "Membership Fees Spring 2024"), amount, and due date.
2.  **Payment (Income):** This is actual money that has arrived in the club's bank account or was handed over in cash. A payment contains the amount, date, and variable symbol.
3.  **Allocation (Matching):** The process by which we **link a payment to a charge**. Only at the moment of allocation is the charge marked as "Paid".

## How it Works in Practice?

The administrator creates a **Charge** (e.g., 2000 CZK for a camp). As a member (or parent), you will see this charge in your profile along with the payment details.
Once you send the money, the administrator records it in the system as a **Payment** and performs an **Allocation** to your charge. This will clear your debt.

## FAQ

### Why do I see "To be paid" in my profile even though I have already paid?
Probably the matching (allocation) of your payment by the administrator has not yet taken place. Synchronization with the bank may not be immediate. If the status lasts longer than a week, contact the club treasurer.

### What is a variable symbol (VS)?
The VS is a unique code by which the system recognizes which member the payment belongs to. You can find the VS for each charge in your member section.

> [!TIP]
> Always provide the correct variable symbol. This will speed up the processing of your payment and avoid unnecessary reminders.
