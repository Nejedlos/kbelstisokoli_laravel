# Evidence and Receipt of Payments

This section is used to record all funds the club receives. Each payment in the system exists independently and is subsequently linked (allocated) to member debts.

## How to Record a New Payment

1.  Go to **Finance -> Payments**.
2.  Click on **New Payment**.
3.  **Payer (Member):** If you know who sent the payment, select them. If not (e.g., VS is missing), leave blank – the payment will be kept as unidentified.
4.  **Amount:** Enter the received amount.
5.  **Receipt Date and Time:** According to the bank statement or cash receipt date.
6.  **Payment Method:** Select Bank Transfer, Cash, or Other.
7.  **Variable Symbol:** If provided, record it for easier lookup.
8.  **Statement Note:** We recommend copying the payer's name from the bank statement (e.g., "John Doe - fees").
9.  **Record Status:**
    - `Recorded`: Default status after entry.
    - `Confirmed`: Mark after check and allocation.
    - `Reversed`: If the payment was returned or entered incorrectly.

## Why Record Payments Separately?

A payment in the system represents a real movement of money. One bank transfer may actually cover multiple things (e.g., a sibling paid for themselves and their brother). By separating the payment from the charge, you can split one payment between multiple debts.

> [!TIP]
> You can insert a unique bank code into the "Transaction ID / Reference" field to prevent duplicate entry of the same payment in the future.
