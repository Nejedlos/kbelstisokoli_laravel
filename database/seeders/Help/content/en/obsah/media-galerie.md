# Gallery and Photo Albums

Visual content from events and matches is the most visited part of our website. Proper organization of albums makes it easier for parents and fans to find photos of their children.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **Content and Media > Gallery**.

## Album Management
Each album represents one event (e.g., a U12 match or a Christmas party).
1. Click **Create Gallery**.
2. Fill in the **Name** and **Event Date**.
3. In the media section, you can bulk upload photos. We support dragging and dropping (Drag & Drop) multiple files at once.

## Working with Photos
- **Order**: You can change the order of photos in the album simply by dragging them with the mouse.
- **Main Photo**: The first photo in the order usually becomes the album's cover photo on the website.
- **Deleting**: You can remove unwanted photos from the album using the trash icon on the preview.

## Media Assets Library
If you need to upload individual files (e.g., a PDF document with club regulations or a logo), use the **Media Assets** section. You can then reference these files in the news text.

## Recommendations
- **Size**: Upload photos in a reasonable resolution (e.g., 1920px on the longer side). The system automatically resizes them for mobile display, but excessively large originals slow down the upload.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of the event for the album – it helps with searching.
