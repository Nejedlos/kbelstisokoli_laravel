# SEO Standards for Editors

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process that makes our club articles and pages more visible in search engines like Google or Bing.

## Where to Find SEO
When editing any news (article), go to the **SEO** tab.

## What to Fill in the SEO Tab
- **Meta Title (Search Title)**: Should contain the most important keywords (e.g., "Basketball recruitment 2026 Prague"). Ideally up to 60 characters.
- **Meta Description (Search Description)**: A short text that people will see in search results. It should entice them to click. Ideally up to 150-160 characters.

## Rules for Writing Articles
1. **Headings**: Use a logical heading structure (H1, H2, H3). The article title is automatically H1. Other subheadings in the text should be H2.
2. **Alt Text for Images**: Always fill in the "Alt Text" (Alternative Description) for uploaded images. Describe what is in the photo (e.g., "U14 junior boys in a match against Liberec"). It helps visually impaired people and search engines.
3. **Keywords**: Put the most important words (basketball, Prague, Sokoli, team name) right into the first sentence of the article.

## Why Is This Important?
Thanks to proper SEO, more people searching for "basketball in Prague" on the internet will find out about our club. Good SEO increases traffic to our website and the club's prestige.
