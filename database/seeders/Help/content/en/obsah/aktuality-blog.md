# News and Club Updates

The club website is the public face of our club for the general public and parents. High-quality and regular news are key to successful presentation.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **Content and Media > News**.

## How to Create a New Article
1. Click the **Create News** button.
2. **Content Tab**:
    - **Title**: The main title of the article.
    - **Category**: Select the appropriate category (e.g., Team A, Youth, Announcements).
    - **Perex**: A short summary that appears on the website's homepage.
    - **Main Content**: The actual text of the article. You can use bold font, headings, and lists.
    - **Main Image**: The article's opening photo. Recommended aspect ratio is 16:9.
3. **Settings Tab**:
    - **Status**: If you want to publish the article immediately, select **Published**. For draft text, leave it as **Draft**.
    - **Featured**: If you mark the article as "Featured," it will be displayed prominently on the website (e.g., in the large top banner).
4. Click **Create** (or Save Changes).

## Editor Tips
- **SEO**: In the SEO tab, fill in the Meta Title and Description. This will help search engines (Google, etc.) find your article more easily.
- **Publication Date**: You can write an article earlier and set a future publication date. The article will appear on the website only at the given time.

## Common Mistakes
- **Too Large Images**: Photos directly from a mobile phone are several MBs. We recommend resizing them (e.g., to a width of 1200px) before uploading to make the website faster.
- **Forgotten Draft**: If you don't see the article on the website, check if you haven't forgotten to switch it from Draft to Published state.
