# Banners and Quick Announcements

Do you need to tell all website visitors something important (e.g., training cancellation or recruitment dates)? Use the announcement system.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **Content and Media > Announcements**.

## Announcement Types
Announcements vary in color and icon according to severity:
- **Info (Blue)**: General information, interesting facts.
- **Success (Green)**: Good news, successes.
- **Warning (Yellow)**: Alerts, changes in dates.
- **Danger (Red)**: Critical news, activity cancellations.

## How to Set Up an Announcement
1. Click **Create Announcement**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and **Message**.
3. Choose the **Type** (determines the color).
4. Set **Valid From** and **Valid To**. The announcement will appear on the website only at the given time and disappear automatically when it expires.
5. Make sure **Active** is checked.

## Where Will the Announcement Appear?
The announcement usually appears as a bar at the top of the website or as a pop-up window, depending on the current website template settings.

## Tips
- **Conciseness**: Users only scan announcements. Put the most important information in the title.
- **Automation**: Schedule an announcement ahead of time (e.g., a Christmas greeting) and the system will publish and withdraw it automatically.
