# Club Partners and Sponsors

Section for managing the logos and links of our partners, which appear in the website footer and on special subpages.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **Content and Media > Partners**.

## Partnership Types
Partners are divided into categories that determine their significance and placement:
- **General Partner**: The most significant partner, usually has the largest logo.
- **Main Partner**: High-priority display partners.
- **Partner**: Standard level of partnership.

## How to Add a Partner
1. Click **Create Partner**.
2. Upload the **Logo** of the partner. Ideally in PNG format with a transparent background.
3. Fill in the **Name** and **Website URL** that the logo should link to.
4. Set the **Priority** (the higher the number, the sooner the partner will appear).
5. Save the changes.

## Technical Recommendations for Logos
- **Format**: PNG or SVG (for maximum sharpness).
- **Transparency**: We recommend logos without a white rectangular background.
- **Color**: If the website footer is dark, consider using a light variant of the logo.
