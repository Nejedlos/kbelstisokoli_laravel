# Partners and Sponsors

The Partners section is used to showcase the companies and institutions that support the Kbelští sokoli club. Proper configuration of a partner ensures their visibility on the website and in printed materials.

### Partner Categories
Partners are divided into categories that determine their priority and placement:
1. **General Partners:** The most important supporters. They are displayed in prominent locations and have the largest logos.
2. **Main Partners:** Significant sponsors.
3. **Other / Media Partners:** Supporting partners.
4. **Institutions:** Local municipalities, sports associations, etc.

### Logo Management
The logo is a key element of the partner's presentation.
- **Format:** We recommend SVG or PNG format with a transparent background.
- **Variant:** If the partner has both light and dark variants of their logo, select the one that fits the website design better (usually the dark logo for a light background).
- **Alt Text:** Don't forget to fill in the company name in the logo description for better SEO.

### Website Display
In the partner details, you can toggle the display in specific areas:
- **Homepage:** The partner will appear in a rotating strip on the main page.
- **Footer:** The logo will be visible at the bottom of every page on the website.
- **Match Badges:** The logo can be displayed in match details as a match partner.

### Priority and Sorting
Using the **Priority** field (a number), you can influence the order in which logos are displayed. A higher number (or lower, depending on the system configuration) moves the partner to the top of the list. General partners should always have the highest priority.

### Frequently Asked Questions
- **Why is the logo not displaying on the website?** Check if the partner is set to "Active" and has at least one placement selected (homepage/footer).
- **Can I set a partner for only one team?** Currently, the partner system is global for the entire club, but you can specify it in the partner description.
