# Photo Pool

The Photo Pool is the central hub for managing club visual content. It is designed for quick uploading, sorting, and publishing of photos to events without the need for complex gallery configurations.

### Bulk Import (Critical)
When uploading a large number of photos, the system uses a **bulk queue**.
- After clicking upload, a **black overlay with a basketball animation** will appear.
- **Important:** Do not close the browser window or navigate away while this is visible. The system is optimizing and saving your photos one by one.
- If you interrupt the import, any remaining photos in the queue will be deleted, and you will need to restart the process.

### AI Caption Enhancement
Each uploaded photo can be automatically analyzed.
- Use the **Regenerate AI** action in the photo details or the table list.
- The system will attempt to recognize the content (e.g., "Player shooting a basket", "Team celebration") and suggest both Czech and English captions.
- These captions are used as `Alt` tags on the website, which improves search engine visibility (SEO).

### Linking to Entities
To display photos correctly, they must be assigned to an entity:
1. **Team:** Photos will be visible on the specific team page on the website.
2. **Event / Match:** Photos will automatically be linked to the selected match or club event (e.g., camp, training).
3. **Featured Photo:** You can mark one photo as "Featured" to serve as a thumbnail/cover image.

### Photographer Tips
- **Format:** Ideally, use JPG or WEBP.
- **Size:** The system automatically resizes photos for the web, but uploading 10MB files unnecessarily slows down the import.
- **Rights:** Only upload photos for which the club has copyright or explicit permission from the author.
