# Branding and E-mail Templates

The Kbelští sokoli system allows you to adapt the appearance of both the administration and the website to the club's color identity.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **System > Branding**.

## What Can Be Configured
- **Club Identity**: Official name, slogan, logos (main, alternative, favicon).
- **Colors (Theme)**: You can choose a color scheme (e.g., Blue, Orange) that will appear on buttons and in navigation.
- **Website Design**: Enable/disable sections such as Sponsors, Partners, or Banners.
- **Social Networks**: Links to Facebook, Instagram, and Youtube in the footer.

## E-mail Templates
Automatic e-mails (invitations, password resets, payment confirmations) adopt the colors and logo from the branding settings.

## Logo Recommendations
- **Size**: Upload logos in higher resolution; the system will resize them automatically.
- **Format**: Use PNG with transparency.
- **Favicon**: Browser icon (32x32px).

## Editing Contacts
In this section, you also manage the technical support e-mail and the bank account information displayed in the member section for payments.
