# Season Restart and Migration

Season restart is the most critical operation in the entire KS system. It facilitates the club's transition into the new competitive year. This action is typically performed in August or September.

### Preparation Before Restart
Before initiating a restart, ensure that:
1. You have created a **new season** in the Seasons section (e.g., 2026/2027).
2. You have finalized finances for the old season (all charges should be paid, cancelled, or transferred).
3. You have a database backup (the system performs this automatically, but caution is advised).

### Procedure on the Season Restart Page
A clear guide is available on the dedicated page under **System > Season Restart**:
1. **Source Season:** Select the concluding season.
2. **Target Season:** Select the newly created season.
3. **Member Migration:** The system automatically generates a "Seasonal Configuration" for each active member in the new season, granting them access to the member section in the new year.
4. **Jersey and Roster Transfer:** If the teams remain unchanged in the new season, the system will transfer players to the corresponding teams (including jersey numbers).

### Impact on Finances
Season restart **DOES NOT** automatically generate new charges. After a restart, you must:
- Navigate to the Finance section.
- Use the **Generate Charges** tool for the new season according to the updated tariffs.
- If members have overpayments from the previous season, the system will automatically account for them during pairing in the new season (if correctly allocated as an overpayment).

### Technical Details
- **Archive:** The old season is automatically set to "inactive." Data (matches, attendance) from it remains, but the website defaults to the new season.
- **Availability:** During the restart, the system may be briefly unavailable to regular users (maintenance mode).

### Common Mistakes
- **Forgotten Teams:** If a team is not continuing in the new season, you must deactivate it or refrain from assigning players.
- **Duplicate Registration:** If a player transferred during the summer, double-check their team link immediately after the restart.
