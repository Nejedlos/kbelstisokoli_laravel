# Season Management and Data Rollover

The Kbelští sokoli system is built on seasons. Every year (typically in August), it's necessary to create a new season and prepare the data for a smooth start.

## Where to Find the Section
In the administration, in the left menu under **Sports Agenda > Seasons**.

## How to Create a New Season
1. Click **Create Season**.
2. Fill in the **Name** (e.g., 2025/2026).
3. **Active**: Only one season can be active at a time. The active season determines which data (attendance, payments) users see in their member section.

## Initialization of Configurations (Important)
When you create a new season, it's empty. For players to start functioning, you must "roll over" their settings from the previous season into the new one:
1. In the list of seasons, click the action icon (copying) for the new season (**Initialize Configurations**).
2. Select the **Source Season** (the previous one).
3. The system will copy all players' settings for tariffs, excuses, and attendance.

## Why Is This Important?
Without a created and activated season, the following will not work:
- Attendance management at training.
- Calculation and prescribing of membership fees.
- Displaying matches in the user's calendar.

## Recommendations
- Create the new season in advance (e.g., in July).
- Until the last August trainings and payments have occurred, keep the old season as the active one.
