The Kbelští sokoli system allows connecting the accounts of parents and their children. This connection is absolutely key for the correct functioning of the member section, excusing from training sessions, and managing family finances.

### How to Connect a Parent with a Child?
The connection is made in the member detail in the People and Members > Users section.
1. Open the detail of the user who is in the **child** role (player).
2. Scroll to the end of the page to the **Parents** section.
3. Click the **Attach** button.
4. Search for the parent's account in the list.
5. If the parent does not yet exist in the system, you must first create a profile for them (see [Member Management and Editing](sprava-clena)).

### Benefits of Connection in the Member Section
The connection brings fundamental convenience to parents when using the mobile application:
- **Profile Switching**: The parent logs in with their e-mail and sees a switcher to the profiles of all their children in the mobile app menu (or the website header). They can thus excuse attendance for them or monitor nominations.
- **Family Wallet**: The parent sees an overview of payments and due contributions for all their connected children in one place.
- **Shared Notifications**: Important messages about training changes or new payments are also received by the parent via e-mail.

### Emergency Contacts
The information about parents in this section serves coaches as an immediate **emergency contact**. In case of an injury at training or a match, coaches have a direct link in the mobile app on the player's profile to call the connected parent.

### Managing and Removing the Connection
You can edit or remove the connection at any time in the **Parents** section of a specific member by clicking the **Detach** action. Detaching the connection does not delete either account; it only terminates their mutual link (e.g., when an adult player becomes independent).
