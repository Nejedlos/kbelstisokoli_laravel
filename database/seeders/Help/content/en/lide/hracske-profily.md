The **Player Profiles** section focuses on the athlete's sporting history and digital identity. Every active player in the system has their history divided by seasons and teams – so-called **Seasonal Configurations** (Stints).

### Seasonal Configurations and History (Stints)
A key concept of the system is preserving the player's history. This data determines which team, in which season, and with which number the player performed.
- **Where to find it**: In the member detail at the bottom of the page (Relation Manager "Seasonal Configurations").
- **What is recorded**: Season, Team, Jersey number (for a specific season), Role in the team (Player/Captain/Coach).
- **Jersey number change**: If a player changes their jersey number during the season, we recommend adding a new record with the new number and validity so that match statistics with the original number remain historically correct.

### Public Player Profile on the Website
If a player has an active "Player Profile" and is assigned to a team in the current season, the system automatically generates a subpage for them on the website kbelstisokoli.cz.
- **Visibility**: Can be influenced globally or individually in settings (toggle "Show on web" in the PlayerProfile model).
- **Data on the web**: Shows the name (or display name), photo (first from the gallery), current team, and jersey number.
- **Statistics**: The system automatically aggregates data from played matches (points, threes, fouls) and attendance and displays them in visual charts and tables on the player's profile.

### Media (Player Gallery)
In the player detail (Player tab), you will find the **Player Photos** section.
- **Primary Photo**: The system always takes the first photo in line as the "profile" photo for the team roster. Photos can be changed simply by drag & drop.
- **Photo Processing**: Uploaded photos are automatically optimized for fast loading on the website and in the mobile application.

### Sporting Results and Aggregation
The player profile serves as a collection point for data from various modules:
1. **Attendance**: Percentage of attendance at training sessions for the current month/season.
2. **Matches**: Number of minutes played, point average, free throws.
3. **Mismatches**: Monitoring "discrepancies" in attendance (reported vs. actual), which helps coaches in evaluating player reliability.

### How to create sporting history for a new player?
1. Open the member detail and the **Player** tab.
2. Turn on **Active Player Profile**.
3. Scroll down to the **Seasonal Configurations** table.
4. Click on **New Seasonal Configuration** and select the current season and team.
5. Set the role to "Player" and enter the jersey number. Save.
6. From this moment on, the player appears in team nominations for matches and trainings.
