# Recruitment and Lead Management

This module is used to record and process new basketball prospects who contact the club through the "I Want to Play" web form.

### What is a Lead?
A Lead is a potential member. In the system, they are displayed under **People and Members > Recruitments**.
- It contains basic information: child's name, year of birth, parent's contact info, and an optional message.
- **Important:** A lead is not yet a user of the system. They do not have access to the member section and cannot be issued payment charges.

### Lead Processing Workflow
Recommended procedure for the recruitment manager:
1. **New:** The lead appears in the list with a status of "New".
2. **Contacting:** The manager calls or writes to the parents. In the system, change the status to "Contacted".
3. **Trial Training:** If the prospect attends training, you can add internal notes to the lead (e.g., "Talented, wants to try").
4. **Decision:**
    - **Accepted:** If the prospect decides to continue, you must **manually create a profile** for them in the Users section.
    - **Rejected / Not Interested:** Mark the lead as "Rejected" or "Archived".

### Converting to a Member (Important)
For security and administrative reasons, the system does not offer a "1-click convert" from Lead to User.
- **Reason:** Creating a member requires significantly more data (address, insurance, GDPR consent) that is not included in the initial form.
- **Procedure:** Open the Lead in one window and create a new User in another. Once the user is created, you can delete or archive the Lead in the system.

### Recruitment Statistics
In the recruitment overview, you can filter by year of birth or season. This helps you plan capacities for individual youth categories.

### Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can a parent fill out a lead?** Yes, the web form is designed to be completed by a legal guardian.
- **Do I receive notifications for a new lead?** Yes, administrators and the recruitment manager receive an email notification for every new prospect.
